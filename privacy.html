Privacy Policy: Vice City Daily Uploader

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The Vice City Daily Uploader is a private tool used only by the Vice City Daily team to upload and schedule videos on our own YouTube channel.

What data it uses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read basic information about our own channel and videos, such as upload status and view counts.

How data is stored

The YouTube login token is stored only on the team's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we do not access any other user's account.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/nicksaris/vice-city-daily.
